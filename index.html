“You wouldn't put 85 octane into a Ferrari.”Anthony, to the Deseret News

I'm a registered dietitian and a trained chef, and for twelve years my job was feeding professional athletes — first as team chef and performance-nutrition assistant for the St. Louis Rams, then running the kitchen for the Utah Jazz from 2018.

That grew into leading Nutrition, Culinary & Hospitality for Smith Entertainment Group: the chefs and dietitians behind the Jazz and the Utah Hockey Club, and the design of the kitchen and dining room for Utah's new NHL practice facility.

Players called my method stealthy-healthy — food that performs and that people actually want to eat. With rookies I ran cooking classes, grocery-store tours and summer visits to their homes. That's the work I do now, for anyone who refuses to settle.
