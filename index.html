About

From the Rams' kitchen to the Jazz's front office.

I started as a team chef and performance-nutrition assistant with the St. Louis Rams. In 2018 the Utah Jazz hired me to run their kitchen; I went on to lead Nutrition, Culinary & Hospitality for Smith Entertainment Group — the chefs and dietitians behind the Jazz and the Utah Hockey Club — and to design the kitchen and dining room for Utah's new NHL practice facility.

Players called my method stealthy-healthy: food that performs, that people actually want to eat. With rookies I ran cooking classes, grocery-store tours and summer visits to their homes. That's the work I do now — for athletes, teams, and anyone who refuses to settle.

“You wouldn't put 85 octane into a Ferrari.”Anthony, to the Deseret News
